Privacy Policy

How we respect and protect your personal information with simple, honest practices.

Last Updated: October 2026

01. What Information We Collect

We only collect basic details you share with us when registering for a session or using our website:

  • Contact Info: Your name, email address, and WhatsApp phone number.
  • Your Score Inputs: The numbers you choose when using our HEW Score tool.
  • Basic Website Data: Simple cookies to help the website load properly on your device.

02. How We Use Your Information

We use your details only to help you get the best experience:

  • To send your live masterclass link and reminder messages before we begin.
  • To send you an invite to our private WhatsApp group for extra resources.
  • To show you your score results and recommended next steps.

03. We Never Sell Your Data

Our Promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal details with anyone else. Your contact information is kept strictly private within HEW Groups.

04. You Can Opt-Out Anytime

You are always in control. Every email we send includes a 1-click unsubscribe button. In our WhatsApp group, you can leave at any time with a single tap.

05. Keeping Your Data Safe

We protect your data using standard encryption and secure servers so your personal information is safe from unauthorized access.
